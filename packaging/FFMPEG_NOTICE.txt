FFmpeg bundled with Pirosman Studio
===================================

Pirosman Studio (MIT OR Apache-2.0) runs FFmpeg as a separate program
(ffmpeg.exe, ffprobe.exe in this folder) to read, preview and export media.
It does not link FFmpeg into its own code. FFmpeg keeps its own license.

What is included
  Program:  FFmpeg 9.0.2, "essentials" static Windows x64 build
  Builder:  gyan.dev (https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/)
  Archive:  https://github.com/GyanD/codexffmpeg/releases/download/9.0.2/ffmpeg-9.0.2-essentials_build.zip
  SHA-256:  60f467265b1e312373dbcd92200c2618a74850f98d3d078e94296bb3fa2047ba
  Files:    ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe, unmodified

also honours the PIROSMAN_FFMPEG_DIR environment variable (advanced use).
