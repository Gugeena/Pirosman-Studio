FFmpeg bundled with Pirosman Studio (Linux)
==========================================

Pirosman Studio (MIT OR Apache-2.0) runs FFmpeg as a separate program
(ffmpeg, ffprobe in this folder) to read, preview and export media.
It does not link FFmpeg into its own code. FFmpeg keeps its own license.

What is included
  Program:  FFmpeg n9.0.2-17-g2a571b6068 (the 9.0 release branch: 9.0.2 plus
            17 fixes), "gpl" static Linux x86_64 build
  Builder:  BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds (https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds)
  Archive:  https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds/releases/download/autobuild-2026-09-30-13-08/ffmpeg-n9.0.2-17-g2a571b6068-linux64-gpl-9.0.tar.xz
  SHA-256:  68ee646831adaae2495618346f3bba94ff207ff83bbd34d643e7004730d66269
  Files:    ffmpeg and ffprobe, unmodified
  Needs:    glibc 2.28 or newer (all other libraries are linked in)
